# Texturas

## grano-halftone.png (2048×2048, en mosaico)
Seamless tileable texture of fine analog film grain mixed with a very subtle halftone dot pattern, neutral grey on black, screen-print look, no visible seams, no text.

## edificios-material.png (2048×2048)
Texture sheet for flat isometric buildings in a two-color screen-print style: off-white (#EEEAE3) matte top surfaces, side faces shaded only with halftone dots (off-white dots on graphite #141418, smaller dots for light faces and larger dots for dark faces), plus one coral (#FF4D2E) halftone variant for the highlighted building. Flat, crisp, no gradients, no text.

**Devolver** con esos nombres. Se usan como texturas de three.js y SVG (Ola 4).
