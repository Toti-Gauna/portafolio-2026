# Texturas

## grano-halftone.png (2048×2048, en mosaico)
Seamless tileable texture of fine analog film grain mixed with a very subtle halftone dot pattern, neutral grey on transparent-looking black background, print screen look, no visible seams, no text.

## islas-material.png (2048×2048)
Top-down texture sheet for stylized floating isometric islands: matte off-white stone top surface (#EEEAE3) and the rocky underside with a printed halftone dot shading in lilac and violet (#C9B8FF, #7B5CFF) on dark navy, clean vector-like, flat shading, no text.

**Devolver** con esos nombres. Se usan como texturas de three.js y SVG (Ola 4).
